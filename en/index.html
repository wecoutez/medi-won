About Us

The doctor who
scans you also
reads the scan

MD, PhD · Cardiology subspecialist · Adjunct professor, Korea University College of Medicine

An echocardiogram is more accurate when the person who performed it also interprets it — the motion observed during the scan is read together with your symptoms, not from images alone. Examination, interpretation and explanation happen in one visit.

  • No referral to another facility just to get tested
  • Results reviewed with you, separating what needs action from what needs watching
  • Partner clinic of the SNUBH Regional CardioCerebroVascular Center — immediate referral in acute cases
